About UROOJ

Idea by Hamza

UROOJ means rising. Created by Hamza Madni under Idea by Hamza, it helps you build discipline, track meaningful habits, and live with greater intention.

Build progress through small, consistent actions. Track tasks and challenges, reflect on patterns, and reset without chasing perfection. Zikr, tasbeeh, gratitude, and muhasabah features support remembrance and accountability.

About AI: AI assisted development and some content; errors are possible. Verify religious quotations and hadith references with reliable scholarly sources.

No guilt over missed days—reflect, reset, and take the next good step in your habits, learning, worship, or daily routine.

Status options→ Choose labels used in task dropdowns

Choose your status labels

Up to 8

These are general fallback labels only. Each task can have its own editable status list in Settings → Edit your daily tasks. For example, Salah uses Takbeer-e-Ula / Jamaat / Ada, while fitness uses Walking / Running / Workout. The combined graph shows every task beside its own current status.

Examples to make daily tracking clear
  • Tasbeeh-e-Fatima: 100 total — 33 SubhanAllah, 33 Alhamdulillah, 34 Allahu Akbar.
  • Qur’an: read your planned pages or verses.
  • Salah: record honestly whether you prayed, missed it, or had a valid excuse.
  • Personal habit: mark it partly done if you made some progress.

Keep labels short so they fit on your phone. “Not recorded” is kept as a required option. Up to 8 labels.

Tap a quick-add option to place it in your list, then save. Duplicate options are ignored.

Your changes are only applied after you save. Existing task-specific options are kept.
