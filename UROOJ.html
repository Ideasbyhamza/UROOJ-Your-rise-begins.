About UROOJ

Idea by Hamza

UROOJ means rising — and that is the heart of this project. Created under Idea by Hamza by Hamza Madni, UROOJ is designed to help people make steady progress in the things that matter: showing up for daily responsibilities, building discipline, leaving unhelpful habits behind, and becoming more intentional with their time.

Real change rarely happens in one big moment. It grows from small actions repeated consistently. UROOJ gives you a place to record your daily tasks, set personal challenges, notice patterns, reflect on your choices, and begin again after a difficult day. Rather than demanding perfection, it encourages honest check-ins, realistic goals, and gradual improvement. Its Islamic self-development features — including zikr, tasbeeh, gratitude, and muhasabah — are intended to support remembrance, mindfulness, and personal accountability.

About AI: UROOJ was built with AI assistance. AI-generated or AI-assisted content can contain mistakes, including in explanations, translations, and references. Please verify religious quotations and hadith references with reliable scholarly sources before relying on them.

The goal is not to make people feel guilty about a missed task. It is to make the next good choice easier to see and act on. Whether you are working to reduce an unwanted habit, overcome procrastination, strengthen a daily routine, focus on learning, or become more consistent in worship and reflection, UROOJ is a companion for tracking the effort and returning to your intention, one day at a time.
